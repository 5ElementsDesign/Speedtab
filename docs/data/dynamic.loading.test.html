Testing fetch URL

YaiTabs can fetch data dynamically via the data-url attribute. Source: dynamic.loading.test.html

Attribute Available
data-theme dark | light
data-color-accent primary | secondary | success | warning | danger | dark | light
data-nav top | right | bottom | left
data-behavior Fade | Slide Up | Slide Down | Slide Left | Slide Right | Zoom | Blur | Flip | Instant
data-auto-accessibility true | false 
<div
  data-yai-tabs
  data-swipe
  data-theme="dark"
  data-color-accent="secondary"
>
  <nav data-controller>
    <button
      data-tab-action="open"
      data-open="1"
      data-url="https://5elementsdesign.github.io/Speedtab/docs/data/dynamic.loading.test.html"
      data-url-refresh=""
      data-min-loading="400"
    >Dynamic 1</button>
  </nav>
  <div data-content>
    <div data-tab="1"></div>
  </div>
</div>
