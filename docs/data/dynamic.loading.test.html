YaiTabs can fetch data dynamically via the data-url attribute. Source: dynamic.loading.test.html

AttributeAvailable
data-theme dark | light
data-color-accent primary | secondary | success | warning | danger | dark | light
data-nav top | right | bottom | left
data-behavior Fade | Slide Up | Slide Down | Slide Left | Slide Right | Zoom | Blur | Flip | Instant
data-auto-accessibility true | false
